Feature Attribution

Feature importances below are proxy attributions based on physicochemical and sequence composition features. These reflect predictive associations in the training data, not experimentally validated causal mechanisms.

Attribution types used in AbDev-X:
• Predictive attribution — SHAP on LightGBM proxy model (PhysChem + Structural sources)
• Residue-level proxy attribution — ESM-2 attention weights mapped to sequence positions (AC-SINS, PR-CHO, Tm assays)
• Structure-mapped feature visualisation — surface hydrophobicity / charge maps via ABodyBuilder3 (requires server-side computation; not shown in demo)
Associations shown are consistent with known assay drivers, not validated mechanisms. "Important feature" means predictive in the GDPa training set, not confirmed biology.
